- The asset is the primary-color, one-line horizontal **marca genérica** lockup (symbol and “Junta de Andalucía” wordmark), suited to a light background. The light-background appearance was checked from the source PNG. No cropping, recoloring, or redrawing was performed.

## Official identity guidance

- Manual and downloadable materials: <https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/identidad/>
- Basic guide: <https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/organismos/presidenciasanidadyemergencias/areas/comunicacion-social/identidad-corporat-jda/paginas/guia-basica-id-corporativa.html>
- The guide describes the Junta identity as a generic mark and an institutional mark. The institutional mark is reserved for representation of the institution and specific formal uses; the generic mark is the everyday-facing identity. The two marks do not normally appear together. The downloaded asset is the generic mark, not the institutional shield lockup.
- The official identity information says logo availability does not itself mean unrestricted permission to use it and directs users to request authorization. Here the mark identifies the support documented by the owner’s supplied award, which expressly requires the article 20 publicity obligations. This is an acknowledgement of that support, not a claim of partnership, endorsement, or certified compliance.

## Acknowledgement wording

The supplied order extract, article 20.1(h), says that information or publicity about the subsidized activity must state that it is subsidized by the Junta de Andalucía department responsible for self-employment. It also calls for non-sexist language and says to follow the Junta Graphic Design Manual.

Spanish copy used on the website, identifying the department named in the award:

> Esta actividad está subvencionada por la Consejería de Empleo, Empresa y Trabajo Autónomo de la Junta de Andalucía.

The supplied award letter names the department as the Consejería de Empleo, Empresa y Trabajo Autónomo. The inspected documents do not prescribe a particular website placement or acknowledgement duration. The order's separate European-funding visibility wording is conditional on European Social Fund Plus funding; the supplied award identifies final-purpose State transfers.
